    What seems to help you

    Each morning the lamp tries one of three gentle wake-ups and notices which ones are followed by steadier mornings and better days for you.

    Going away?

    Hold the green button on the lamp for 3 seconds to say you're away, and again when you're back. Days away are never counted.

    What your clinic sees

    Shared with your clinic

    • Your mood each morning, and missed days
    • When your day starts
    • When the plant is watered
    • Daylight in the room

    Never shared

    • Temperature and humidity
    • Anything from a camera or microphone: the lamp has neither
    • Where you are: away mode only says "away"

    The lamp flags worrying behaviour, but the clinic decides on the course of action. It never diagnoses you.

    Need help now?

    Nobody watches the lamp in real time. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, please call someone now.

    Emergency: 112 MIELI Crisis Helpline: 09 2525 0111

    MIELI's line is in Finnish and open day and night. MIELI doesn't charge for the call; your operator's normal rate applies. You can also contact your own clinic.
