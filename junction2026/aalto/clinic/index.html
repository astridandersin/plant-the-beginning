Who to check in with this week

Demo with simulated patients, generated through the lamp's real logic. Never use this page with real patient data.

Patients

    Ranked by need: "check in" first, then "keep an eye", then steady. Tested on real wristband data: about 9 % of steady people reach "check in" by mistake in a week, and about 19 % "keep an eye".

    First activity each morning

    The first button press, watering or movement. Shaded band: within an hour of the person's own usual time for that kind of day (weekdays and weekends differ). Amber: far from usual, earlier or later. Grey: away.

    Morning mood

    From the check-in buttons, 1 low to 5 great. A gap is a missed check-in.

    Plant care

    Average soil moisture per day; drops mark watering.

      What the lamp is learning

      Each day the lamp picks one of three gentle responses and scores the day by how steady the morning and mood were. Bars: its estimate so far, with uncertainty. It favours what seems to suit this person, and keeps trying the others.
